TBT Water + Light Lab v1

Rule changes are applied together on reset, keeping each run internally consistent.

World and display

The canvas keeps the requested cell aspect ratio and shrinks to fit the screen if necessary. Horizontal space wraps; sky and bottom do not. Plant hue is a deterministic genome signature, useful for following exact inherited forms but not a calibrated genetic-distance scale.

Light and shade

The uppermost cell receives 10/10 vertical energy. The three values set the relative energy below one, two, or at least three plant-cell layers. Exposure and vertical shade are independent and additive when both are enabled.

Conserved water cycle

Every particle is always exactly one of: free liquid, freely diffusing vapor, or held by one plant cell. A connected plant can conduct stored water to a photosynthesizing cell. Rate 1 transpiration releases one held particle on every energy event; rate 0 retains it. A zero timeout disables that timeout. Surface evaporation closes the conserved cycle without creating rain.

Evolution and lifespan

Mutation remains 60% bit flip, 20% subtree duplication, and 20% subtree deletion. Frequency dependence rejects locally common exact genomes at germination.
