World and display

Opacity controls update live and affect only rendering, not physics. Seed opacity covers planted seed cells and traveling seeds; at 0, planted seeds look like ordinary plant cells and traveling seeds disappear. Light and shade

The uppermost cell receives 10/10 vertical energy. Each of the three lower-layer values remains independently editable from 0 to 10; the buttons are only shortcuts. Exposure and vertical shade are independent and additive when both are enabled.

Conserved water cycle

Every particle is always exactly one of: free liquid, freely diffusing vapor, or held by one plant cell. A connected plant can conduct stored water to a photosynthesizing cell. Rate 1 transpiration releases one held particle on every energy event; rate 0 retains it. A zero timeout disables that timeout. Surface evaporation closes the conserved cycle without creating rain.

Evolution and lifespan

The default is one initial seed. If initial plants is greater than one, the first uses the selected position and the rest start randomly. Mutation remains 60% bit flip, 20% subtree duplication, and 20% subtree deletion. Frequency dependence rejects locally common exact genomes at germination.

What this OEE diagnostic tests

Each exact whole genome is treated as one activity component. Once every 1,000 ticks, the lab compares the persistence of real genomes with a neutral shadow that receives the same ordered births and deaths but assigns genome identity without selection, drawing uniformly from genomes discovered so far.

How to read the sparkline

The line is the median normalized cumulative activity among genomes alive at that sample. Green/positive means the typical live genome has accumulated more sampled persistence than its neutral counterpart; coral/negative means less. This is not one overall “OEE score.”

If the median settles around +1 to +2 indefinitely, selection is real but bounded: the typical genome stays only one or two sampled persistence units ahead of neutral chance. That fails the unbounded-median requirement. A candidate OEE trajectory would be noisy, but its sustained envelope would keep reaching materially higher regimes—for example roughly +1, then +3, then +10, then +30 as observation time grows—without settling into a fixed band.

Total versus new activity

Raw total and normalized total must also keep reaching higher sustained scales rather than repeatedly returning to the same range. New activity is different: it only has to stay persistently above zero. New activity hovering around +1 can satisfy that one condition if genuinely new genomes keep clearing the noise floor; it does not rescue a median or total that plateaus.

What a strong single run would look like

  • Raw total activity keeps establishing higher long-run levels.
  • Normalized total and the median sparkline both keep expanding upward after chance is removed.
  • New activity remains above zero through late windows instead of appearing only in an early burst.
  • Extending the run reveals continued expansion, not a rise followed by a stable band or decline.

What the activity-statistics stage requires

  • Raw total cumulative activity keeps increasing without a finite ceiling.
  • Total and median activity remain unbounded after neutral normalization.
  • Normalized activity attributable to genuinely new genomes stays persistently positive.

A single finite run can look consistent with OEE but cannot prove unboundedness. Repeated seeds and progressively longer/larger tests must show that the apparent upward envelope does not saturate. Passing these activity trends is also not the whole OEE claim: ongoing complexity growth and indefinite scalability still need separate evidence.
