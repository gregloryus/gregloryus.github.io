Thicket 01

Every plant carries one heritable number, its signature, drawn as its hue. Some tissue is a mouth: it takes energy from a touching cell of another plant, but only when that cell's signature matches the mouth's inherited key. The same number decides who you can eat and who can eat you.

Leaf tissue earns from open space around it, so income and crowding are the same quantity with opposite signs. Mouth tissue earns nothing from space. Rind earns nothing and cannot be eaten. Bloom tissue earns nothing, hoards, and spends to make a seed.

Everything starts from one ancestor genome: a single stalk ending in a bloom. Branching, mouths, rind, defence, and every signature but the first have to be invented by mutation.
