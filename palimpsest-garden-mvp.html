Compact rule card
  1. Light enters from above. Living surfaces with water turn it into dissipating energy.
  2. Matter and water never appear from nothing. Matter becomes bodies, corpses, then substrate again.
  3. Only visibly competent cells evaluate a small heritable decision tree. Successful extension differentiates the parent and creates a new competent frontier.
  4. Rules can only extend, fork, invest, wait, or detach. They contain no leaf, root, seed, species, or ecological-role labels.
  5. Matching face tags conduct local resources regardless of ancestry. Touching strangers can graft or tap one another.
  6. Shade, maintenance, wear, support loss, death, falling, and decay reopen gaps. Dead structures retain tags while history slowly weathers away.
  7. Detached competent cells mutate their decision trees locally. Reproductive success is the only selection process.
