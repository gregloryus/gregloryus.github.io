How this prototype works

Bright tips are the only developmental agents. They move one lattice cell at a time, leaving green conduit behind. Touching cells of the same genet form the transport graph; there are no parent pointers or central plant object.

Blue packets enter through wet buried tissue. With the water cycle on, a blue packet reaching exposed tissue can transpire into vapor and leave a red energy packet behind. Both colors perform ordinary random walks through adjacent same-genet tissue. A tip briefly latches one of each; the red is spent on an action and the blue is released back into the network.

Each tip carries the same sixteen-entry reactive genome as its seed. Local air/soil, crowding, wetness, and one internal phase bit select an action: extend, turn, bud, seek openness or water, or invest in a seed. Mutation occurs only when seeds are made in this first version.

Conduit stays saturated only when both red and blue have recently passed. Unused paths fade into dead wood, then crumble into organic soil that holds water longer.
