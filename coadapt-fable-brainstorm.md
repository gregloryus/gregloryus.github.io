# coadapt — Fable brainstorm (2026-11-12)

Context: Greg asked for big ideas, not maintenance. Source of truth remains U21–U23. Everything
below is optional suggestion in the U24 sense — the next instance should treat these as one
possible reading, free to find its own. Nothing here has been run.

---

## 1. The synthesis: this whole project's progress curve is one variable — lineage fidelity

Lay the findings end to end and a single law explains all of them:

- Stage 1 only produced coadaptation under **repeated local pairing** (spatial fidelity).
- Stage 2's grip law only worked once bodies were **stereotyped** (muTree 0.05 — morphological
  fidelity: a shape persistent enough to be a target). cA doubled.
- Every failure (upkeep cascade, nursery gates, obligate collapse ×3) is the same failure:
  a law that *demanded* more partner fidelity than the world could yet supply.

The general claim: **the depth of coadaptation is bounded by the expected number of repeated
interactions between the same two lineages across generations.** This is the lineage-timescale
version of the iterated-prisoner's-dilemma precondition — cooperation needs a shadow of the
future; coadaptation needs a shadow of the future *between genealogies*. Stage 1's pairing and
Stage 2's stereotypy were both fidelity boosts; the metrics doubled each time fidelity rose.

This gives an a-priori screen for any proposed law: *ask how it changes expected lineage-level
interaction fidelity before running it.* Laws that raise it should help; laws that spend it
(partner-dependence, gating) are only affordable after it exists. The recurring failure pattern
("obligate cannot precede sorted") is a corollary, not a separate law.

And it predicts the next move, because the ladder has a known top rung that hasn't been built:

## 2. Co-dispersal: let evolved couples become heritable (the headline mechanism)

Nature's solution to "how do obligate partners find each other" is **vertical transmission**:
lichens disperse soredia carrying both fungus and alga; leafcutter queens carry the fungus;
the endpoint of the same ladder is endosymbiosis (mitochondria). The documented natural route
is: facultative → vertically transmitted → *then* obligacy can evolve, because a partner is now
guaranteed at birth. That is precisely the missing middle rung between the current facultative
regime and the obligate "world of couples" that keeps collapsing when forced directly.

Mechanism sketch (graded, survivable alone — obeys the failure law):
- When an organism seeds **while holding an embrace ≥ k paired faces**, with probability
  `pCoDisperse` (an evolvable gene, initialized ~0) the seed is a **joint diaspore** carrying
  both partners' genomes; on germination it establishes the two bodies side by side in their
  matched orientation.
- Cost dials: heavier seed (e.g. 2× seedCost split between partners), shorter dispersal.
- Control: `pCoDisperse` frozen at 0 reproduces today's world exactly.

Why this is not "prescribing the relationship": the channel is offered to *whatever embrace
evolution has already produced*; which lineages use it, with whom, and whether it pays is
selected, not authored. The emergence question moves up a level and stays falsifiable: **do
co-dispersing pairs outcompete solo dispersers?**

Predicted effects: couples become founders, so worlds start *reading* as couples (the aesthetic
gap closes for free); cA should jump qualitatively, not incrementally, because assortment is now
inherited rather than re-discovered each generation; sibling crowding is partly mooted (joint
diaspores arrive pre-matched, so dock-site competition among kin matters less — this subsumes
the queued "sibling dispersion" item). And if it wins, the sim has recapitulated the first step
toward symbiogenesis — co-lineages, genealogies that travel and diverge together, U21's "they
are each other's environment" made literally heritable.

## 3. Why fit doesn't *look* like fit: shape needs a second master

The legibility theory. A hummingbird bill and a flower corolla read as "fit like a glove"
because each shape is mostly explained by its *own* livelihood — flight, nectar-guarding —
and the fit is a visible, costly **deviation** from each partner's solo optimum. In coadapt,
matching is shape's *only* job, so evolved fit is noise conforming to noise: statistically
real, visually arbitrary. Complementarity is only legible against a backdrop of independent
functional constraint.

Godsroll had that backdrop — the vertical light economy — and the coadapt line deliberately
removed it to isolate exchange. The synthesis move: **recombine them.** Light from above gives
every body a non-relational shape objective (broad, high, canopy-efficient — T2-safe: light is
metabolic, not matched). The exchange economy then pulls shapes *away* from that optimum,
toward partners. Plants visibly bending and reaching toward their partners against a light
gradient is the canonical image of coevolution.

This also yields the metric that actually operationalizes U21, better than cA:
**solo-optimum deviation.** Evolve (or compute) each lineage's shape alone; in the paired
world, measure the direction of its deviation. Coadaptation = partners' deviation vectors
point toward each other. "Shaped to each other" becomes a measured quantity: mutual
deformation, not just adjacency statistics.

## 4. Chiral kernel: make the twirl perpetual (and the palette seasonal)

The antipodal reward is symmetric, so it has exactly two stable attractors — the world
resolves into two teams and *stops*. Replace it with a **directed offset**: benefit flows
from hue h to hue h+Δ (e.g. Δ=120°). Then:

- T1 holds automatically (Δ≠0 ⇒ nothing matches itself or kin).
- Relationship becomes a *directed graph* — chains and cycles, emergent trophic structure.
  Δ=120° makes trios the natural closed loop: U23's "A's waste feeds B feeds C feeds A"
  emerges rather than being installed.
- There is no stable team-split, so the hue distribution can never settle: the Red Queen
  regime, U21's "twirl in this spiral coevolution" made literal and *permanent*. Signature
  metric: global hue angular momentum vs a symmetric-kernel control.
- Aesthetically: the whole world's palette slowly rotates over thousands of generations —
  seasons of color at evolutionary timescale, visible in a time-lapse.

(If Stage 1's demonstrated "twirl" already used a chiral kernel, this item is "port it to
bodies as the default metabolism," which is still unexplored.)

## 5. Phase genes: synchrony is perceptually loud where shape-fit is subtle

Possibly the best effort-to-payoff idea here. Add a world day-cycle and one gene φ: the phase
at which an organism runs its emission/absorption (and perhaps seeding). To receive, you want
a partner emitting while you absorb — so the rewarded relation is a **complementary phase
offset**: another circle, with anti-reflexivity built in (kin share φ, and same-phase is
worthless — T1 for free, exactly like hue). The entire Stage-1 machinery, metrics, and
fixtures port verbatim to this second circular trait.

Why it matters: the legibility problem with spatial fit is that humans must zoom and squint;
but humans detect *synchrony* effortlessly and at a glance (fireflies). Evolved partners
pulsing in coordinated rhythm across the field read as "in relationship" with zero
instruction. It is also the classic coevolution axis (phenology: flowering time ↔ pollinator
emergence), and the day-night cycle it requires is simultaneously the single cheapest cozy
feature (ambient light, quiet nights).

## 6. Enclosure-weighted grip: make crevices literal

One-line sharpening of the grip law toward U23's "complementary pieces that fit within some
crevice": weight each shared face by how *enclosed* the contact is — e.g. the count of
partner cells in the 8-neighborhood of the contact cell. Flat side-by-side contact earns the
floor; a cell seated in a pocket earns the ceiling. Graded (no obligate cliff), and it drives
evolution of literal sockets and plugs — interlocking silhouettes instead of parallel ones.
Metric: distribution of contact-enclosure depth vs the unweighted control.

## 7. Resolving the two-minds problem: author the stage, never the inhabitants

U23's tension ("embrace the artificial" vs "the whole novelty is that I don't") dissolves
once you ask *which layer* is authored. Cozy ecosystem games author the creature/relationship
layer; that's why they can't surprise you. Science sims author the physics layer. The craft
is choosing the layer you refuse to touch. So: go **maximalist on authored beauty in the
container** — terrain, gravity and sky, day/season cycles, palette, rendering, sound, the
rituals of viewing — while keeping the relationship layer 100% emergent. A garden is exactly
this: a designed vessel for undesigned life. The novelty claim survives intact.

Concrete presentation-layer moves, none of which touch the laws:

- **Render the flux.** Per-interface flux is already computed; draw it as a soft breathing
  glow along matched interfaces. The economy becomes visible light; relationships become
  visible *tonight*, before any new law is written. Cheapest legibility win available.
- **The co-history film.** U21's "more beautiful than could have been designed" is beautiful
  because the shape *carries history* — and history is only visible with time-depth. The
  snapshot/rewind/lineage machinery in coadapt-2 can already reconstruct any couple's past:
  select an embrace → auto-play the two lineages' mutual shaping across generations. A
  courtship told in ten thousand ticks. This is the single feature that directly *exhibits*
  the U21 dream rather than measuring it.
- **The naturalist's notebook.** Generate a field guide from the sim itself: every persistent
  form gets a page — portrait, range, partner list from the flux ledger, lineage notes from
  snapshots. The sim writes its own nature journal; the player's stance becomes discovery,
  not design. (Click-select + lineage chain is already most of the plumbing.)
- **Signs of metabolism.** Biophilia responds to visible life-*process*, not green pixels:
  growth spurts, senescence color shifts, decay, regrowth after damage, seasonal quiet.
  Rendering state the sim already has as process the eye can read.
- **Reroll as the gardener's verb.** The possible-history mechanic, reframed: the player
  never touches a relationship — their only power is to dream alternate pasts and watch the
  ghost. Contemplation of contingency as the core loop. The machinery exists; it's a framing,
  not a feature.

## Suggested sequencing (if any of this is adopted)

1. **Render the flux** (hours, no laws touched) — buys legibility immediately and makes every
   later experiment easier to read by eye.
2. **Phase gene + day cycle** (small; Stage-1 machinery ports) — second coadaptation axis,
   synchrony legibility, cozy lighting in one move.
3. **Co-dispersal** (the big swing; graded, controlled, falsifiable) — the fidelity ladder's
   top rung and the predicted qualitative jump from mosaic to couples.
4. **Light economy recombination + solo-optimum deviation metric** — the legibility theory
   test; heavier, but it's the path to fit that *looks* like fit.
5. Chiral kernel and enclosure-weighted grip as independent one-dial experiments alongside.

Each respects the house rules: graded and survivable alone, control one dial away, pass
criteria predeclarable (cA jump for #3; deviation-vector alignment for #4; angular momentum
for the chiral kernel; enclosure depth for the grip variant).
